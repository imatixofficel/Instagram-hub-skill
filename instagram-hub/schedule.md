# Module: Schedule

**One job:** build a fixed weekly posting schedule from the user's view hours.

## Input needed

- Hours or days when posts get views / followers are most active (from Insights)
- The user's time zone
- Niche (for sensible posting frequency)

## Rules

- Output specific days and exact times, not vague ranges.
- Schedule posts 15–30 minutes before the peak so the post is fresh when activity rises.
- Suggest a realistic number of posts per week for the niche, mixing formats (Reels, carousels, single images, Stories).
- Do not stack all posts in the same slot; spread them across the user's best windows.
- If the data is thin, say the schedule is a starting point and tell the user to re-check Insights after 2–3 weeks and adjust.

## Output

A weekly table:

| Day | Time | Format |
|---|---|---|

Followed by two or three lines explaining why these slots were chosen, based on the user's own data.
