# Module: Hashtags

**One job:** give exactly 5 hashtags for each post.

## Rules

- **Exactly 5** hashtags per post. Not 4, not 6.
- Mix: 1 broad niche tag, 2 mid-size tags, 1 specific long-tail tag, 1 location or community tag when relevant.
- Match them to the actual content of that specific post, not generic filler.
- Use real hashtags in the user's language and market.
- Do not repeat the same 5 on every post; vary them.
- Do not claim follower counts, reach or guaranteed views. If you need real numbers, verify by searching, or say you can't confirm them.
- Never use banned, spammy or misleading hashtags.

## Output

For each post: the 5 hashtags on one line, plus a one-line reason for the choice if the user asks.
