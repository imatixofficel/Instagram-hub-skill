# Module: Stories (times, covers, content)

**One job:** plan what to post in Stories, when to post it, and how the Story/Highlight covers should look.

## Input needed

- Niche, audience, time zone
- Active hours of followers (from Insights) and, if available, Story views/replies/taps data
- Current Highlight covers (from the screenshot), if any

## Story posting times

- Base the times on the user's own active hours. Do not copy generic "best times" from the internet.
- Spread Stories across the day in 2–3 bursts instead of posting everything at once:
  - Burst 1: morning, to catch people checking their phone early.
  - Burst 2: about 30–60 minutes before the main peak, to warm up the audience before a post or Reel goes live.
  - Burst 3: at or just after the peak, to catch people who are most active.
- Suggest a realistic daily count (usually 3–7 Stories) the user can keep up for a month.
- Give exact times in a table: Day, Time, Story type.
- Say plainly that this is a starting point, and to compare Story views per time slot in Insights after 2 weeks and adjust.

## What to post in Stories

Mix these types across the week, based on the niche:

- Behind the scenes / real daily moments (not staged)
- Quick tips or a mini lesson (1–3 frames)
- Poll, quiz, question box or slider (one per day at most, so it does not feel spammy)
- Proof: real customer messages, results, reviews (only with permission; never invented)
- "New post / new Reel is live" teaser with a clear reason to tap
- Personal or story-style frames to build trust

For each Story idea give: the frame-by-frame outline, the text on screen, the sticker/interaction to use, and the best time slot.

## Story and Highlight covers

- One consistent look: 2–3 colors and one font family matching the profile.
- Highlight cover: simple icon or one short word, readable at tiny size, centered so the circle crop does not cut it.
- Story frames: big readable text, enough contrast, important content inside the safe area (leave the top and bottom ~14% free for the interface).
- Give the user concrete specs: Story size 1080×1920 px (9:16), Highlight cover about 1080×1920 with the icon centered, and suggest simple tools they can use.
- Highlight titles: short, searchable words (1–2 words).

## Output

1. Story time table.
2. 5–7 Story ideas with outlines.
3. Cover recommendations (colors, font, icon ideas for each Highlight).
